# Collaboration policy

- Label implemented behavior, experiments, and proposals so readers know what they can try on the stated version.
- Share only material that maintainers can inspect without access to a private system. Use redacted logs and screenshots, or a small synthetic dataset when real data cannot be shared.
- Keep credentials, customer records, proprietary data, and private traces out of public reports.

For report fields, see [Contact and feedback](contact-us.md). For pull requests, use the [repository contribution guide](../../../CONTRIBUTING.md).
